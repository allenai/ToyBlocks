Sim-to-real Toolkit

Trained in simulation, tested on a real arm

We trained Molmo 2 (high level) and ManiFlow (low level) only on ToyBlocks data, with no real-world demonstrations, and ran them on a Franka FR3 with a Robotiq gripper.

Results

We tested two things: single skills with ManiFlow (pick, place, flip and rotate), and full three-letter spelling tasks with Molmo 2 + ManiFlow. Real-world runs used thirty scene configurations with 4–10 blocks that appear in the training data.

SimulationReal robot
ManiFlow, single skills80.0%81.1%
Molmo 2 + ManiFlow, three-letter spelling55.5% (111/200)50.0% (15/30)

Single-skill success is about the same in simulation and on the real robot. The spelling runs used the easiest setting, with every letter visible and upright, and 30 real trials is not many, so we need more trials before reading much into the gap.

More detail in the blog post.

How the policy runs

Molmo 2 looks at the front camera and decides what to do next: which skill, and a point in the image that marks the block. ManiFlow takes that command and controls the arm. Both were trained on renders with heavy domain randomization (lighting, backgrounds, table materials, camera poses), which is a big part of why the real lab doesn't look out of place to them.

What's in the repo

The code that drives the physical robot isn't part of this release. The simulation side is: generated environments start from a calibrated copy of our real workcell.

  • The sim2real scene is the default scene for every generated environment. It reproduces the real cell, including calibrated front-camera extrinsics and intrinsics, and datasets record it as their scene_id. Scene and robot flags
  • fr3_robotiq_gripper_sysid is an FR3 with a Robotiq gripper and system-identified joint armature and friction values. Robot config
  • --use-calibrated-cameras is on by default in evals and gives every environment the real cameras' intrinsics. Cameras in evaluation
